# Code of Conduct

Agent Playbook is an open source learning community. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive
- Welcome beginners and their questions
- Give feedback about the work, not the person
- Respect different backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints
- Protect private information
- Credit other people's work
- Help keep discussions focused and useful

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or demeaning language
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Deliberately unsafe or malicious contributions
- Repeated disruption of discussions or reviews
- Presenting copied work as your own

## Reporting concerns

Please report conduct concerns privately to the Dunia Hub maintainers.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
