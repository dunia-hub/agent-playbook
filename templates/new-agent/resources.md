## External services and costs

The default offline path uses no external service. Groq is optional. If you add
another API, model, database, wallet, blockchain, messaging service, or paid
tool, document:

- Why it is required
- What data is sent
- What permissions it receives
- Whether it can create side effects
- Current free-tier limits and possible costs
- A free or offline testing path

Do not place secrets in documentation, examples, fixtures, source code, or tests.
