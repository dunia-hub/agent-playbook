## Research Guidance

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources when possible.
- Compare publication dates, methods, evidence, limitations, and incentives.
- Treat search snippets as discovery aids, not evidence.
- Open the cited source and verify important claims before using them.
- Report conflicts and missing evidence instead of forcing certainty.
