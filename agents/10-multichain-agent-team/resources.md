## Safety concepts

- **Plan only:** the team can inspect a proposed operation but cannot execute it.
- **Least authority:** no specialist has a wallet, secret, signer, or send method.
- **Independent checks:** each ecosystem has its own address and balance logic.
- **Dependency-aware consensus:** downstream work is blocked when a prerequisite fails.
- **Data freshness:** stale or future-dated snapshots cannot pass strict policy.
